# 四级英语写作模板与技巧

## 第一章 议论文三段式结构总览

大学英语四级写作中，议论文（Argumentative Essay）是最常见的体裁。一个标准的议论文采用**"总-分-总"三段式结构**：

| 段落 | 功能 | 字数参考 |
|-----|------|---------|
| 开头段（Introduction） | 引出话题 + 表明观点 | 30-50 词 |
| 正文段（Body） | 分点论证 + 举例/对比/因果 | 80-100 词 |
| 结尾段（Conclusion） | 总结观点 + 展望/建议 | 30-40 词 |

**总字数要求**：四级写作要求不少于 120 词，建议控制在 150-180 词之间。

## 第二章 开头段写作模板

开头段的核心任务有两个：
1. **引入话题（Hook）**：吸引读者注意，可以描述现象、引用数据或提出问题
2. **表明立场（Thesis Statement）**：一句话清晰地表明你的观点

### 2.1 现象描述类开头模板

#### 模板 1：社会现象引入
```
Nowadays, the phenomenon of ____________（话题） has aroused wide concern among the public. 
According to a recent survey, ____________（用简单数据支撑）. 
From my perspective, ____________（你的观点）.
```

**例句 1**（网购话题）：
> Nowadays, the phenomenon of online shopping has aroused wide concern among the public.
> According to a recent survey, over 75% of Chinese consumers prefer shopping online to visiting physical stores.
> From my perspective, the advantages of online shopping outweigh its disadvantages.

**例句 2**（低头族话题）：
> Nowadays, the phenomenon of phubbing（低头症） has aroused wide concern among the public.
> According to a recent survey, the average adult spends over 4 hours daily on their smartphones.
> From my perspective, excessive smartphone use poses a serious threat to our health and social relationships.

#### 模板 2：争议性话题引入
```
In recent years, there has been a heated debate on whether ____________（争议问题）. 
Some people argue that ____________（反方观点）, while others firmly believe that ____________（正方观点）. 
After weighing the pros and cons, I side with the latter/latter view, because ____________（理由概述）.
```

**例句 1**（是否应鼓励私家车）：
> In recent years, there has been a heated debate on whether private car ownership should be encouraged.
> Some people argue that private cars bring convenience and freedom, while others firmly believe that they cause serious traffic congestion and air pollution.
> After weighing the pros and cons, I side with the latter view, because environmental protection should take priority over individual comfort.

**例句 2**（大学生是否应该兼职）：
> In recent years, there has been a heated debate on whether college students should take part-time jobs.
> Some people argue that part-time jobs take up too much study time, while others firmly believe that they offer valuable work experience.
> After weighing the pros and cons, I side with the latter view, because practical experience is as important as academic learning.

### 2.2 观点引入类开头模板

#### 模板 3：直接点明话题 + 观点
```
When it comes to the topic of ____________（话题）, people's opinions vary from person to person. 
As far as I am concerned, ____________（你的观点）. My reasons are as follows.
```

**例句**（时间管理的重要性）：
> When it comes to the topic of time management, people's opinions vary from person to person.
> As far as I am concerned, learning to manage time effectively is one of the most important skills for college students.
> My reasons are as follows.

## 第三章 正文段论证模板

正文段是文章的核心部分，需要用充分的论据支撑观点。常用的论证方式有三种：
- **因果论证**（Cause and Effect）
- **对比论证**（Comparison and Contrast）
- **举例论证**（Exemplification）

### 3.1 因果论证模板

#### 模板 4：分点说明原因
```
There are several reasons why ____________（重申观点）. 
First and foremost, ____________（原因一）. For example, ____________（补充小例子）. 
Furthermore, ____________（原因二）. A typical example is that ____________（举例）. 
Last but not least, ____________（原因三）. 
```

**例句**（为什么要保护环境）：
> There are several reasons why environmental protection is of great importance.
> First and foremost, a clean environment is essential for our physical health. For example, air pollution has been linked to various respiratory diseases such as asthma.
> Furthermore, preserving the ecosystem helps maintain biodiversity. A typical example is that many endangered species are now on the brink of extinction due to habitat destruction.
> Last but not least, sustainable development ensures a better future for the next generation.

#### 模板 5：影响分析（利/弊）
```
____________（某现象） has brought about many positive/negative effects on our lives. 
To begin with, ____________（影响一）. 
In addition, ____________（影响二）. 
Most importantly, ____________（影响三）. 
```

### 3.2 对比论证模板

#### 模板 6：利弊对比
```
Like a coin with two sides, ____________（话题） also has both advantages and disadvantages. 
On the one hand, ____________（优点）. For instance, ____________（举例）. 
On the other hand, however, ____________（缺点）. A case in point is that ____________（举例）. 
Despite its drawbacks, I still believe that the benefits outweigh the problems, because ____________（你的判断）.
```

**例句**（网络教育的利弊）：
> Like a coin with two sides, online education also has both advantages and disadvantages.
> On the one hand, online learning offers great flexibility and convenience. For instance, students can learn from anywhere at any time without being limited by geographical distance.
> On the other hand, however, online education lacks face-to-face interaction and requires strong self-discipline. A case in point is that many students fail to complete their online courses due to a lack of supervision.
> Despite its drawbacks, I still believe that the benefits outweigh the problems, because technology can help bridge the educational gap between urban and rural areas.

### 3.3 举例论证模板

#### 模板 7：单个详细例子
```
My viewpoint can be best illustrated by the example of ____________（例子主体）. 
____________（详细叙述例子过程）. 
This example clearly demonstrates that ____________（例子所支持的观点）.
```

**例句**（坚持的重要性）：
> My viewpoint can be best illustrated by the example of J.K. Rowling, the author of Harry Potter. Before achieving success, she was a single mother living in poverty, and her manuscript was rejected by 12 publishers. However, she never gave up her dream and kept writing. Eventually, the Harry Potter series became one of the best-selling books in history.
> This example clearly demonstrates that perseverance is the key to success.

#### 模板 8：多个小例子排比
```
Numerous examples can be found in our daily life. 
Take ____________（例子一） as an example; ____________（说明）. 
Another case in point is ____________（例子二）; ____________（说明）. 
All these examples show that ____________（总结观点）.
```

## 第四章 结尾段总结模板

结尾段要简洁有力，不要引入新的话题，主要功能是**总结观点**和**升华主题**。

### 4.1 总结重申类模板

#### 模板 9：重申 + 建议
```
From what has been discussed above, we may safely draw the conclusion that ____________（总结观点）. 
Therefore, it is advisable for us to ____________（具体建议）. 
Only in this way can we ____________（倒装句升华展望）.
```

**例句**（环境保护）：
> From what has been discussed above, we may safely draw the conclusion that environmental protection is an urgent task for every individual.
> Therefore, it is advisable for us to take practical actions such as saving water, reducing waste, and using public transportation.
> Only in this way can we build a greener and more sustainable world.

#### 模板 10：展望未来式
```
In summary, while ____________（承认不同声音）, I firmly believe that ____________（重申观点）. 
Looking ahead, ____________（展望未来发展）. 
With joint efforts, we are confident that ____________（美好展望）.
```

**例句**（人工智能话题）：
> In summary, while some people worry that AI may replace human workers, I firmly believe that AI will ultimately benefit humanity by freeing us from repetitive tasks.
> Looking ahead, AI will continue to transform industries from healthcare to education.
> With joint efforts, we are confident that AI will lead us into a new era of progress and prosperity.

## 第五章 常用过渡词分类

过渡词（Transition Words）就像文章的"粘合剂"，能让逻辑更清晰、行文更流畅。按功能分类如下：

### 5.1 表示顺序 / 列举

| 类别 | 常用过渡词 |
|-----|-----------|
| 首先 | First and foremost / To begin with / First of all / For one thing |
| 其次 | Furthermore / Moreover / In addition / Besides / For another / Second |
| 最后 | Last but not least / Finally / Eventually |

**使用示例**：
> There are three reasons why I prefer studying abroad. First and foremost, studying overseas allows me to experience a different culture. Furthermore, it helps improve my language proficiency more quickly. Last but not least, it broadens my horizons and helps me make friends from all over the world.

### 5.2 表示转折 / 对比

| 类别 | 常用过渡词 |
|-----|-----------|
| 然而 / 但是 | However / Nevertheless / Nonetheless / Yet / But |
| 相反地 | On the contrary / In contrast / By contrast |
| 另一方面 | On the other hand / Conversely |
| 尽管如此 | Despite this / In spite of that |

**使用示例**：
> Living in a big city has many advantages, such as more job opportunities and better public facilities. However, it also has drawbacks like high housing prices and traffic congestion. By contrast, life in the countryside is much quieter and more relaxed.

### 5.3 表示因果

| 类别 | 常用过渡词 |
|-----|-----------|
| 因为（原因） | Because of / Due to / Owing to / On account of / As a result of |
| 因此（结果） | Therefore / Thus / Hence / Consequently / As a result |
| 所以 | For this reason / Accordingly |

**使用示例**：
> Due to the outbreak of COVID-19, many universities had to shift to online teaching. As a result, students had to adapt to a new learning environment with greater self-discipline.

### 5.4 表示举例

| 常用过渡词 |
|-----------|
| For example / For instance |
| To illustrate / As an illustration |
| A case in point is that ... |
| Take ... as an example |
| Namely / Specifically / In particular |

**使用示例**：
> Many celebrities have used their influence to promote charity. A case in point is that Yao Ming has been actively involved in wildlife protection, calling for an end to the illegal trade of ivory.

### 5.5 表示总结

| 常用过渡词 |
|-----------|
| In conclusion / To sum up / In summary / In short / In brief |
| From what has been discussed above |
| On the whole / All in all |
| As has been noted / As mentioned above |

### 5.6 表示强调

| 常用过渡词 |
|-----------|
| In fact / As a matter of fact / Actually |
| In particular / Particularly / Specifically |
| Above all / Most importantly |
| Undoubtedly / Apparently / Obviously |
| It is worth noting that ... |

## 第六章 作文常见加分技巧

### 6.1 使用高级词汇替换

将基础词汇替换为更高级的表达，给阅卷老师留下好印象：

| 基础词汇 | 高级替换 |
|---------|---------|
| important | vital / crucial / essential / significant / indispensable |
| good | excellent / outstanding / remarkable / positive / beneficial |
| bad | negative / adverse / harmful / undesirable |
| many | numerous / a great number of / a host of / a multitude of |
| some | several / a number of / various |
| think | hold the view that / argue / maintain / claim / contend |
| very | extremely / incredibly / remarkably / considerably |
| get | obtain / acquire / attain |
| help | assist / aid / facilitate |
| use | utilize / employ / make use of |
| more and more | increasingly / an increasing number of |
| about | approximately / regarding / concerning |

**对比示例**：
- ❌ Very good: It is very important to protect the environment.
- ✅ Better: It is vitally significant to protect our environment.

### 6.2 使用多样句式结构

避免全篇都是简单句，穿插以下句式：

#### 倒装句（Only/否定词开头）
```
Only in this way can we solve the problem effectively.
Seldom do we realize the importance of health until we lose it.
```

#### 强调句（It is/was ... that/who ...）
```
It is diligence that ultimately leads to success.
It was in college that I discovered my passion for programming.
```

#### 状语从句（although, as, since, unless, so...that）
```
Although the task is challenging, we will try our best to complete it.
The book is so interesting that I couldn't put it down.
```

#### 定语从句（which, who, where, when）
```
Those who are willing to help others are usually happy people.
Reading is a good habit, which benefits us in many ways.
```

#### 非谓语动词
```
Having been translated into many languages, the book became popular worldwide.
Compared with traditional shopping, online shopping is more convenient.
```

### 6.3 万能谚语/名言（适量使用）

恰当引用谚语可以为文章增色，但不要滥用。以下是几个通用的谚语：

1. **关于坚持**：Rome was not built in a day.（冰冻三尺非一日之寒）
2. **关于行动**：Actions speak louder than words.（事实胜于雄辩）
3. **关于准备**：Opportunity favors the prepared mind.（机会偏爱有准备的人）
4. **关于实践**：Practice makes perfect.（熟能生巧）
5. **关于态度**：Attitude is everything.（态度决定一切）
6. **关于学习**：Never too old to learn.（活到老，学到老）

**使用示例**：
> As the old saying goes, "Opportunity favors the prepared mind." Therefore, we should make every effort to prepare ourselves for future challenges.
