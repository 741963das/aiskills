# 英语语法常见错误精讲

## 第一章 时态混用

时态是英语中最基础也最容易出错的知识点。很多同学在写作和口语中时态随意切换，导致逻辑混乱。

### 1.1 一般现在时 vs 一般过去时

#### 典型错误场景

❌ **错误**：Last week, I go to the park with my friends.
✅ **正确**：Last week, I **went** to the park with my friends.
（解析：有明确的过去时间状语 last week，必须用一般过去时。）

❌ **错误**：The teacher said that the earth was round.
✅ **正确**：The teacher said that the earth **is** round.
（解析：客观真理、科学事实在任何从句中都用一般现在时。）

❌ **错误**：He is liking playing basketball.
✅ **正确**：He **likes** playing basketball.
（解析：表示心理状态和情感的动词（like, love, hate, know, want, believe 等）一般不用于进行时态。）

#### 一般现在时的使用场景总结

| 场景 | 示例 |
|-----|------|
| 习惯性、经常性动作 | I get up at 7 every day. He often plays football. |
| 客观事实/真理 | The sun rises in the east. Water boils at 100°C. |
| 能力/特征 | She speaks three languages. This knife cuts well. |
| 按时间表发生（将来） | The train leaves at 8:30 tomorrow. |
| 标志词 | always, usually, often, sometimes, seldom, never, every day/week |

#### 一般过去时的使用场景总结

| 场景 | 示例 |
|-----|------|
| 过去某时发生的动作 | I visited my grandmother yesterday. |
| 过去一段时间内的习惯性动作 | He lived in Beijing for 10 years. |
| 标志词 | yesterday, last week/year, ago, in 2020, just now |

### 1.2 现在完成时 vs 一般过去时

这是中国学生最容易混淆的一组时态！

#### 核心区别

| 时态 | 侧重点 | 时间联系 | 典型标志词 |
|-----|-------|---------|-----------|
| 一般过去时 | 动作发生在**过去某个确定时刻**，只谈过去 | 与现在无关 | yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago |
| 现在完成时 | 过去发生的动作对**现在造成影响或结果** | 与现在有关 | already, yet, just, ever, never, since, for, so far, in recent years |

#### 典型错误对比

❌ **错误**：I have seen the movie yesterday.
✅ **正确**：I **saw** the movie yesterday.（有 yesterday，用一般过去时）
✅ **正确**：I **have seen** the movie three times.（强调到目前为止看了三次，与现在有关）

❌ **错误**：I study English for 5 years.
✅ **正确**：I **have studied** English for 5 years.（持续到现在的动作，用现在完成时）

❌ **错误**：I have been to Paris last summer.
✅ **正确**：I **went** to Paris last summer.
✅ **正确**：I **have been** to Paris twice.（强调到目前为止去过两次）

#### have been to vs have gone to

| 表达 | 含义 |
|-----|------|
| have been to | 去过某地（人已回来） |
| have gone to | 去了某地（人还在那边，未归） |

例句：
- She has been to Japan three times.（她去过日本三次，现在在本地）
- She has gone to Japan for a meeting.（她去日本开会了，现在还在日本）

### 1.3 过去完成时的使用

#### 核心概念："过去的过去"

过去完成时 = had + 过去分词，表示在**过去某个时间之前**就已经完成的动作。

必须有一个"过去的参考点"，比这个参考点更早的动作才用过去完成时。

❌ **错误**：I had finished my homework.（单独使用无意义，缺少参考时间）
✅ **正确**：I **had finished** my homework **before** my parents came back.
（父母回来 came 是过去，完成作业在回来之前，即"过去的过去"）

✅ **正确**：By the end of last term, we **had learned** 2,000 English words.
（上学期末是过去时间点，学单词在此之前）

#### 常见搭配

- by + 过去时间点：By 9 o'clock last night, we had already arrived.
- before / after + 一般过去时句子：After he had finished his work, he went home.
- 间接引语中：She said that she had seen the film before.

## 第二章 主谓一致

主谓一致指谓语动词在人称和数上必须和主语保持一致。三大原则：**语法一致、意义一致、就近原则**。

### 2.1 语法一致原则

主语是单数 → 谓语用单数；主语是复数 → 谓语用复数。

#### 易错点 1：第三人称单数（三单）

❌ **错误**：He go to school by bus every day.
✅ **正确**：He **goes** to school by bus every day.

❌ **错误**：Tom and Jack is good friends.
✅ **正确**：Tom and Jack **are** good friends.（两个人，复数）

> 规则：一般现在时中，主语是第三人称单数（he/she/it/单数名词），谓语动词加 s/es。

#### 易错点 2：主语后接修饰语不影响谓语形式

主语 + with / together with / along with / as well as / like / but / except / besides / including 等短语时，谓语形式与**最前面的主语**一致。

❌ **错误**：The teacher, together with his students, are going to visit the museum.
✅ **正确**：The teacher, together with his students, **is** going to visit the museum.
（核心主语是 the teacher，单数）

❌ **错误**：No one except his parents know the truth.
✅ **正确**：No one except his parents **knows** the truth.
（核心主语是 no one，单数）

#### 易错点 3：不定代词作主语

下列不定代词作主语时，谓语用**单数**：
- each, either, neither
- one, everyone, everyone, somebody, nobody, nothing, anything, everything
- each of / either of / neither of + 复数名词

❌ **错误**：Everyone in our class like English.
✅ **正确**：Everyone in our class **likes** English.

❌ **错误**：Neither of the answers are correct.
✅ **正确**：Neither of the answers **is** correct.

### 2.2 意义一致原则

形式上是单数但表示复数意义，或形式上是复数但表示单数意义，按意义判断。

#### 集合名词

| 集合名词 | 表示整体（单数） | 表示成员（复数） |
|---------|----------------|----------------|
| family | My family is a big one. | My family are watching TV. |
| team | The team has won the game. | The team are all working hard. |
| class | Our class is on the third floor. | The class are taking notes. |
| government / police / public | 通常指整体或成员 | （根据语境判断） |

> 特别注意：police 和 people 作"人们"讲时，谓语永远用复数：
> ✅ The police are searching for the suspect.
> ✅ Young people like pop music.

#### 以 s 结尾但实为单数的名词

| 类别 | 例子 | 谓语 |
|-----|------|------|
| 学科名词 | maths, physics, politics, economics, news | 单数 |
| 国名/地名 | the United States, the United Nations, the Philippines | 单数 |
| 疾病名称 | measles（麻疹）, mumps（腮腺炎） | 单数 |

✅ **正确**：Physics **is** a difficult subject.
✅ **正确**：The United Nations **was** founded in 1945.

#### 时间/金钱/距离/重量作主语

表示总量时，视为整体，谓语用**单数**：

✅ **正确**：Ten thousand dollars **is** a large sum of money.
✅ **正确**：Three years **is** not a long time.
✅ **正确**：500 kilometers **is** a long distance.

### 2.3 就近原则

谓语形式与**最靠近**它的主语保持一致。适用于以下结构：

| 结构 | 例句 |
|-----|------|
| there be 句型 | There is a pen and three books on the desk. |
| either ... or ... | Either you or he is wrong. |
| neither ... nor ... | Neither the teacher nor the students were satisfied. |
| not only ... but also ... | Not only the students but also their teacher enjoys the movie. |
| or | Tom or his brothers are to blame. |

❌ **错误**：There are a book and two pens on the desk.
✅ **正确**：There **is** a book and two pens on the desk.
（a book 最靠近 be 动词，单数）

## 第三章 冠词误用

冠词（a/an/the）是英语中最小的词，但也是最容易用错的。

### 3.1 a vs an

区别不在于字母是元音还是辅音，而在于**发音的音素**：

| 用 a（辅音音素开头） | 用 an（元音音素开头） |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| a university（/j/） | an hour（/aʊ/，h 不发音） |
| a European country（/j/） | an honest man（/ɒ/，h 不发音） |
| a one-year plan（/w/） | an umbrella（/ʌ/） |
| a book / a tree | an apple / an egg / an orange |

> 易错词：a useful tool, a usual day, an S, an X-ray, an 8-year-old boy

### 3.2 定冠词 the 的使用

#### 必须用 the 的情况

1. **特指**：the book on the desk, the man in blue
2. **独一无二的事物**：the sun, the moon, the earth, the sky
3. **序数词/最高级前**：the first time, the tallest boy
4. **姓氏复数前，表示全家人**：the Smiths（史密斯一家）
5. **形容词前，表示一类人**：the poor（穷人）, the rich（富人）, the old
6. **江河湖海/山脉群岛**：the Yangtze River, the Pacific Ocean, the Alps
7. **乐器前**：play the piano / the guitar / the violin
8. **固定搭配**：in the morning, at the same time, by the way, on the whole

❌ **错误**：He is tallest boy in our class.
✅ **正确**：He is **the** tallest boy in our class.

❌ **错误**：I like to play piano in my spare time.
✅ **正确**：I like to play **the** piano in my spare time.

### 3.3 零冠词（不加 the）的情况

#### 不用冠词的情况

1. **不可数名词/复数可数名词表泛指**：Water is important. Books are our friends.
2. **星期/月份/季节/节日前**：on Monday, in June, in summer, on Christmas Day
3. **三餐前**：have breakfast/lunch/supper/dinner
4. **球类/棋牌运动前**：play basketball / football / chess
5. **学科/语言前**：study English, learn Chinese, major in history
6. **by + 交通工具**：by bus / by car / by bike / by plane
7. **固定搭配**：at home, in bed, at school, in trouble, on foot, go to hospital（看病）

❌ **错误**：I usually have the breakfast at 7 am.
✅ **正确**：I usually have breakfast at 7 am.

❌ **错误**：He goes to school by the bike every day.
✅ **正确**：He goes to school by bike every day.

#### 对比：有无冠词的差异

| 短语 | 含义 |
|-----|------|
| go to school | 去上学（学生） |
| go to the school | 去那所学校（办事） |
| in hospital | 生病住院 |
| in the hospital | 在医院里（工作/探访） |
| at table | 在用餐 |
| at the table | 在桌子旁边 |

## 第四章 介词搭配错误

介词是英语中最灵活的小词，用法非常多，常考的固定搭配需要记住。

### 4.1 时间介词：in / on / at

| 介词 | 用法 | 示例 |
|-----|------|------|
| **at** | 具体时刻；节假日 | at 7 o'clock, at noon, at night, at Christmas, at the age of 18 |
| **on** | 具体某一天；某天的上午/下午/晚上 | on Monday, on May 1st, on Sunday morning, on a cold night |
| **in** | 较长时段（月份、季节、年份、世纪）；早上/下午/晚上 | in May, in spring, in 2024, in the 21st century, in the morning |

❌ **错误**：I was born in July 1st, 2000.
✅ **正确**：I was born **on** July 1st, 2000.

❌ **错误**：He usually gets up on 6:30.
✅ **正确**：He usually gets up **at** 6:30.

### 4.2 地点介词：in / on / at

| 介词 | 用法 | 示例 |
|-----|------|------|
| **at** | 较小地点/门牌号/站点 | at home, at the station, at the door, at No. 168 Main Street |
| **in** | 较大地点/空间内部 | in Beijing, in the room, in the park, in the world |
| **on** | 在某个平面/道路上 | on the wall, on the desk, on the second floor, on Green Street |

✅ The shop is at the end of the street.
✅ He lives in Shanghai.
✅ The picture is on the wall.

### 4.3 常见动词介词搭配错误

#### 动词 + for

❌ **错误**：I paid the book 20 yuan.
✅ **正确**：I paid 20 yuan **for** the book.

其他常见搭配：
- **look for** 寻找：I'm looking for my keys.
- **wait for** 等待：He is waiting for the bus.
- **ask for** 请求：You can ask for help.
- **care for** 关心/喜欢：She cares for her family.
- **search for** 搜寻：They searched for the missing child.

#### 动词 + about

❌ **错误**：He didn't tell me his plan.
✅ **正确**：He didn't tell me **about** his plan.（也可以 tell sb sth，但 tell sb about sth 更常用）

常见搭配：
- **talk about** 谈论
- **think about** 考虑
- **worry about** 担心
- **complain about** 抱怨
- **know about** 了解

#### 动词 + of

- **dream of** 梦想：He dreams of becoming a doctor.
- **think of** 想到/认为：What do you think of the idea?
- **hear of** 听说：I've never heard of that place.
- **take care of** 照顾：She takes care of her little brother.
- **consist of** 由...组成：Water consists of hydrogen and oxygen.

#### 动词 + to

❌ **错误**：I prefer tea than coffee.
✅ **正确**：I prefer tea **to** coffee.

常见搭配：
- **belong to** 属于：This book belongs to me.
- **happen to** 发生：What happened to you yesterday?
- **listen to** 听：Listen to the teacher carefully.
- **refer to** 参考/指的是：Please refer to the dictionary.
- **get used to** 习惯于：He has got used to the life here.

#### 动词 + from

- **prevent/stop/keep ... from doing** 阻止...做：We must stop him from making such a mistake.
- **benefit from** 受益于：I benefited a lot from the lecture.
- **different from** 与...不同：Your plan is different from mine.
- **suffer from** 遭受：Many people suffer from stress.

#### 动词 + into

- **divide ... into** 把...分成：Please divide the students into 4 groups.
- **translate ... into** 翻译为：Translate the sentence into English.
- **turn into** 变成：Water turns into ice at 0°C.

### 4.4 形容词介词搭配

| 形容词 + 介词 | 例句 |
|--------------|------|
| be **interested in** | I am interested in music. |
| be **good at** | He is good at playing football. |
| be **afraid of** | Don't be afraid of making mistakes. |
| be **angry with sb** | She was angry with her brother. |
| be **angry about sth** | He was angry about the result. |
| be **proud of** | Parents are proud of their children. |
| be **full of** | The bottle is full of water. |
| be **familiar with** | I'm familiar with this city. |
| be **familiar to** | The name is familiar to me. |
| be **similar to** | Your idea is similar to mine. |
| be **famous for** | Beijing is famous for the Great Wall. |
| be **responsible for** | He is responsible for the project. |

## 第五章 定语从句引导词选择

定语从句是语法考试的重点，也是写作中的加分项。但 which / that / who / whom / whose / when / where / why 很容易混淆。

### 5.1 关系代词 vs 关系副词

| 关系词 | 先行词 | 在从句中作的成分 |
|-------|-------|----------------|
| **who** | 人 | 主语/宾语 |
| **whom** | 人 | 宾语（正式） |
| **whose** | 人/物 | 定语（"...的"） |
| **which** | 物 | 主语/宾语 |
| **that** | 人/物 | 主语/宾语（不能引导非限定性定语从句） |
| **when** | 时间名词 | 时间状语 |
| **where** | 地点名词 | 地点状语 |
| **why** | reason | 原因状语 |

**判断技巧**：先把从句单独拿出来，看缺少什么成分。
- 缺主语/宾语/定语 → 用关系代词
- 不缺主干（主谓宾完整）→ 用关系副词

### 5.2 典型错误对比

#### 错误 1：缺关系代词或关系代词误用

❌ **错误**：This is the book I bought yesterday it.
✅ **正确**：This is the book **which/that** I bought yesterday.
（从句 I bought 缺宾语，用 which/that，或者省略）

❌ **错误**：The students don't pass the exam will be punished.
✅ **正确**：The students **who/that** don't pass the exam will be punished.
（从句 don't pass 缺主语，who/that 不可省略）

#### 错误 2：that 与 which 的误用

**只能用 that，不能用 which 的情况**：

1. 先行词是 all, everything, nothing, anything, little, much, none 等不定代词：
   ✅ **Everything that** he said is true.

2. 先行词被 all, any, every, no, the only, the very, the same, the last 修饰：
   ✅ This is **the only book that** I need.

3. 先行词被序数词或最高级修饰：
   ✅ This is **the best film that** I have ever seen.

4. 先行词既有人又有物：
   ✅ They talked about **the teachers and schools that** they had visited.

**只能用 which，不能用 that 的情况**：

1. 引导非限定性定语从句（有逗号隔开）：
   ✅ He **passed the exam, which** made his parents happy.（which 代指前面整句话）

2. 介词 + 关系代词：
   ✅ This is the house **in which** Lu Xun once lived.

#### 错误 3：关系代词 vs 关系副词混淆

❌ **错误**：I will never forget the day which we met.
✅ **正确**：I will never forget the day **when** we met.
（从句 we met 主谓完整，缺时间状语 → 用 when）
✅ **正确**：I will never forget the day **which/that** we spent together.
（从句 we spent 缺宾语，spent the day → 用 which/that）

❌ **错误**：This is the village where I visited last year.
✅ **正确**：This is the village **which/that** I visited last year.
（从句 I visited 缺宾语，visited the village → 用 which/that）
✅ **正确**：This is the village **where** I was born.
（从句 I was born 主谓完整，缺地点状语 → 用 where）

#### 错误 4：whose 的误用

❌ **错误**：This is the girl which father is a teacher.
✅ **正确**：This is the girl **whose** father is a teacher.
（"这个女孩的父亲"，表示所属关系 → whose）

## 第六章 非谓语动词

非谓语动词包括：**不定式（to do）**、**动名词（doing）**、**分词（doing/done）**。是语法中的难点。

### 6.1 不定式 vs 动名词作宾语

#### 只能加 doing 作宾语的动词（V + doing）

常见动词口诀：**考虑建议盼原谅，承认推迟没得想，避免错过继续练，否认完成就欣赏，禁止想象才冒险，不禁介意准逃亡**

| 动词 | 英文 |
|-----|------|
| 考虑、建议、期待、原谅 | consider, suggest/advise, look forward to, excuse/pardon |
| 承认、推迟、避免 | admit, delay/put off, avoid |
| 错过、继续、练习 | miss, keep/keep on, practice |
| 否认、完成、欣赏 | deny, finish, enjoy/appreciate |
| 禁止、想象、冒险 | forbid, imagine, risk |
| 不禁、介意、允许/逃脱 | can't help, mind, allow/permit, escape |

✅ I suggest **going** out for a walk after dinner.
✅ Would you mind **opening** the window?

#### 只能加 to do 作宾语的动词（V + to do）

常见动词口诀：**三个希望两答应，两个要求莫拒绝，设法学会做决定，不要假装在选择，同意志愿加计划**

| 动词 | 英文 |
|-----|------|
| 希望、答应 | hope/wish/expect, agree/promise |
| 要求、拒绝 | demand/ask, refuse |
| 设法、学会、决定 | manage, learn, decide/determine |
| 假装、选择 | pretend, choose |
| 同意、志愿、计划 | agree, volunteer, plan/intend |

✅ I hope **to see** you again.
✅ He decided **to study** abroad.

#### 即可加 to do 又可加 doing，但意义差别大

| 动词 | + to do | + doing |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **remember** | 记得要去做（未做） | 记得做过（已做） |
| **forget** | 忘记要去做（未做） | 忘记做过（已做） |
| **regret** | 遗憾要做（未做） | 后悔做过（已做） |
| **stop** | 停下来去做另一件事 | 停止正在做的事 |
| **try** | 努力去做 | 尝试做（试试） |
| **mean** | 打算做 | 意味着 |
| **can't help** | 不能帮助做 | 忍不住做 |

✅ I remembered **locking** the door, so I went back to check.（记得锁过门）
❌ 不对！应该是：I didn't remember **locking** the door, so I went back to check.
✅ I remembered **to lock** the door before I left.（记得要去锁，然后锁了）

### 6.2 分词作定语/状语的选择

#### 现在分词（doing）vs 过去分词（done）作定语

核心判断：看被修饰的名词和动词之间是主动还是被动关系。
- **主动关系 → doing**（被修饰的名词发出这个动作）
- **被动关系 → done**（被修饰的名词承受这个动作）

❌ **错误**：a breaking cup（a broken cup 才对，杯子"被打碎"，被动）
❌ **错误**：a moved story（a moving story 才对，故事"令人感动"，主动）

✅ **a sleeping** baby = a baby who is sleeping（主动，宝宝发出睡觉的动作）
✅ **a written** report = a report which is written（被动，报告被写）

总结：
- 情感类动词（interest, excite, surprise, disappoint, move, tire, bore, confuse 等）
  - 描述事物（令人...的）→ interesting / exciting / surprising / disappointing
  - 描述人（感到...的）→ interested / excited / surprised / disappointed

#### 现在分词 vs 过去分词作状语

分词作状语时，看**句子主语**和分词的关系：
- 主语主动发出 → doing
- 主语被... → done

❌ **错误**：Seeing from the top of the mountain, the city looks beautiful.
（主语是 the city，city 不会"看"，不能主动发出 see 的动作）
✅ **正确**：**Seen** from the top of the mountain, the city looks beautiful.
（城市"被看"，用过去分词 seen）

✅ **正确**：**Walking** along the street, I met an old friend.
（主语 I 主动发出 walking 的动作）
